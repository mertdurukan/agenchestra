# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). A short summary follows; the linked text is authoritative.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, empathetic and constructive.
- Give and accept feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the community.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, trolling or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome advances.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for harmful behavior.

To report a problem, use the repository's private GitHub security advisory form (Security tab, "Report a vulnerability") and mark the report as a conduct concern, or open an issue asking a maintainer to contact you privately without including sensitive details. Reports are handled confidentially and fairly.
